RenderUpdate SMAA sources and lookup textures come from libretro/slang-shaders,
pinned commit 53deb2efcb79f4fc3e94e9602d5974b4b64e8f94:
https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders/tree/53deb2efcb79f4fc3e94e9602d5974b4b64e8f94/anti-aliasing/shaders/smaa
Wrappers retain their SPDX-License-Identifier: Unlicense headers.
SMAA.hlsl comments/whitespace were normalized from Windows-1252 to UTF-8/LF for the loader;
shader code and algorithms are unchanged. Lookup textures are unchanged.
SMAA attribution and license are preserved below.
stock.slang and adaptive-sharpen.slang are CC0-1.0 HakuX-Advanced examples.
Presets adapt upstream smaa.slangp paths and optionally append our sharpen pass.
